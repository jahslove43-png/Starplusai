How it works

From customer invitation to approved social proof.

The workflow is intentionally simple: create a request, collect feedback, moderate it, then publish only what is approved.

01

Add a customer

Store the customer information needed for a review invitation in your business workspace.

02

Create a review request

Choose a customer and create a unique review link. Requests expire after the configured 30-day window.

03

Send the invitation

The authenticated review-request function validates ownership before sending an email through Resend.

04

Customer submits feedback

The public review function validates the token, rating, comment, expiration and completion state before creating a pending review.

05

Business moderates

Approved, rejected and pending states keep the publication decision explicit.

06

Widget displays approved reviews

The public widget feed exposes approved review content and a complete approved-review count and average rating.
