Privacy information

This page is a product placeholder and should be reviewed by the business's legal adviser before being treated as a complete privacy notice.

Data handled by the product

StarPlusAI currently processes account information, business profile information, customer information supplied by a business, review-request records, review content and email-delivery events.

Authentication

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are not stored manually by the application.

Public review content

Only approved review information is intended for public display. Customer information should be limited to what is necessary for the widget.

Legal review required

Retention periods, data-subject rights, subprocessors, international transfers and jurisdiction-specific disclosures should be documented before production legal publication.
