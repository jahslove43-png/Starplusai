How long is the free trial?+

The current trial period is 3 days and is controlled by application data.

Do I need a credit card?+

No credit card is required to create an account and begin the current trial.

Which reviews appear on the widget?+

Only approved reviews are exposed through the public widget. Pending and rejected reviews are not published.

Can a review request be submitted twice?+

No. A completed review request cannot be used for another submission.

How long are review links valid?+

The current review-request workflow uses a 30-day expiration period.

Is paid checkout active?+

Not yet. The displayed plans are planned pricing tiers and do not charge customers.

Is Google login active?+

Google OAuth is supported when configured in Supabase, but it should only be considered active when that configuration is verified.

How are emails handled?+

Authentication and product emails use server-side email infrastructure. Private API credentials are not placed in the public website.
